Lutz’s Law.

The bigger the pull request, the further away the merge.

In open source, as the size of a pull request grows, its probability of being merged tends toward zero.

I coined this on October 21, 2021, because I had provided so many examples of oversized pull requests getting stuck in review. Credit also goes to Adrin Jalali for helping me get them unstuck.

Originally about human review in open source. Internal PRs and AI change the picture.
